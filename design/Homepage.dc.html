How it works

STEP 01
Write the question

Type it, or start from a template. Multiple choice, ranked, rating or reactions.

STEP 02
Share it

A link, a QR code or a six-character code. Or send each member their own private link for a verified vote. Voters never need an account.

STEP 03
Watch it land

Results update live. When you’re done, download the record as a PDF or CSV for the minutes.

What you get

Australian hosting

Your polls, votes and member data are hosted in Australia. No overseas processing.

Anonymous or verified voting

Keep responses anonymous, or send one private link per member for an election or motion.

Results record

Download a PDF or CSV with the question, options, eligible voters, counts and open and close times.

Live results for the room

Bar, pie or donut, updating the moment a vote lands.

Private polls

Password protection, time limits and member lists.

Works on any phone

No app to install, and no account needed to vote.
